Project context

MediCore Health Distribution Ltd. is a pharmaceutical distribution company supplying medicines and healthcare products to hospitals, pharmacies, clinics and other healthcare providers. The practice dataset represents sales activity across distributors, customers, sales regions, products, sales representatives and teams.

Project focus

My work on this project focused on reviewing and cleaning the raw sales dataset using Microsoft Excel Power Query, preparing a more consistent and structured table for potential downstream use.

Data preparation work

  • Imported and reviewed the supplied raw sales records in Power Query.
  • Inspected the available fields, column names and data types.
  • Applied data-cleaning and transformation steps to prepare the records.
  • Reviewed the resulting cleaned table in Excel.

The supporting evidence for this exercise consists of the MediCore company introduction and the cleaned Excel table. These demonstrate the business context and the prepared dataset.
