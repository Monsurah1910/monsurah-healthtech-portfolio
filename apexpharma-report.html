07. ApexPharma Logistics Ltd.: Pharmaceutical Sales & Performance Analysis

Pharmaceutical distribution · Sales analytics · Excel · Interactive dashboard

A data analytics capstone exploring how cleaned pharmaceutical sales records can be transformed into commercial insights. The project examined revenue performance, product demand, regional activity, distributor contribution and sales-team effectiveness to support more informed management decisions.

Business context and problem statement

ApexPharma Logistics Ltd. distributes medicines and healthcare products to retail chains, diagnostic laboratories, urgent care centres and community pharmacies across four major commercial regions. As the business expands, management needs a reliable view of sales performance and its commercial drivers.

The project brief identified inconsistent distributor, sales-representative and regional records as barriers to understanding product performance, customer segments and regional bottlenecks. The analysis was intended to support resource allocation, sales-team evaluation and commercial growth planning.

Project objective

Clean and analyse the pharmaceutical sales dataset in Excel, identify meaningful sales patterns and build an interactive management dashboard that allows performance to be explored by month, drug category, region, distributor and sales team.

Dataset and analytical scope

The working dataset contained 350 sales transactions and 14 fields, including transaction ID, order date, month, distributor, customer name and type, sales region, product name, drug category, quantity, unit price, revenue, sales representative and sales team.

My contribution: Data preparation, analysis and dashboard development

I worked through the analysis workflow from initial data review to the interactive Excel dashboard. My activities included:

  • Reviewing the dataset and field definitions to understand the available sales information and identify data-quality issues.
  • Using Power Query and Excel cleaning techniques to address inconsistencies, standardise categories and text fields, resolve missing customer-type information, and check data types.
  • Validating revenue values against the relationship between quantity and unit price.
  • Using PivotTables to analyse sales by month, product and drug category, distributor and region.
  • Developing an Excel dashboard with summary KPIs, charts and interactive slicers to support management exploration of the sales results.
  • Interpreting the results and formulating evidence-based recommendations for commercial review.

Key findings from the analysis

The completed analysis produced the following headline results:

Highest revenue monthAugust · ~$23.71M
  • Monthly performance: August recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately $23.71 million, while May was the lowest at approximately $6.94 million.
  • Drug categories: Antimalarials generated $69,086,700, approximately 40.8% of total revenue. Antibiotics contributed $39,236,400. Together, Antimalarials and Antibiotics accounted for approximately 64% of revenue.
  • Leading products: Artesunate 60mg Injection generated approximately $38.93 million; Artemether/Lumefantrine 80/480mg approximately $30.15 million; and Ciprofloxacin 500mg approximately $21.37 million. Together, these three products contributed approximately $90.46 million, or 53.4% of total revenue.
  • Distributor performance: Biohealth Logistics recorded the highest distributor revenue at approximately $46.60 million; Medexpress Supply recorded the lowest among the distributors analysed at approximately $38.45 million.
  • Regional performance: The West region recorded the highest revenue at approximately $47.93 million, while the North region recorded the lowest at approximately $38.43 million.

Customer-type and individual sales-representative/team rankings are not stated here because their verified comparative figures are not included in the available final findings. The dashboard includes these dimensions for further exploration.

MonthDrug CategorySales RegionDistributorSales Team

The dashboard is intended to help management move from headline performance to more focused questions about product demand, regional contribution and distributor or team performance.

Business recommendations

  • Review product availability and demand planning: use the strong revenue contribution from Antimalarials and Antibiotics, alongside product-level trends, to inform stock planning and availability reviews.
  • Investigate monthly variation: examine the factors behind August's higher revenue and May's lower revenue before making changes to procurement, inventory or sales targets.
  • Focus regional performance reviews: use the West and North results as starting points for understanding differences in customer demand, distribution coverage and sales execution.
  • Monitor distributor contribution: review distributor performance alongside service, fulfilment and coverage measures rather than relying on revenue alone.
  • Use the dashboard for ongoing review: refresh the underlying data regularly and use the slicers to support consistent monthly and regional performance discussions.

Outcome and learning

The capstone resulted in a cleaned and structured sales dataset, a set of PivotTable analyses, an interactive Excel management dashboard and a group of quantified commercial insights. It strengthened my practical ability to prepare imperfect data, validate key measures, analyse business performance and communicate findings through dashboard reporting.

Project context and integrity: This was a 10Alytics training capstone using the supplied ApexPharma Logistics Ltd. dataset. The figures above are analytical results from that dataset, not audited company accounts or evidence of changes implemented by a live pharmaceutical distributor. Recommendations are proposed areas for management review, not measured business outcomes.
