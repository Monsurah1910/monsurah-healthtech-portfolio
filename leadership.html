Leadership & Impact

My leadership journey has been shaped by engineering practice, professional service and a commitment to creating opportunities for women, girls and communities. Through APWEN Lagos Chapter and public-sector engineering roles, I have contributed to professional development, technical engagement, quality and community initiatives.

APWEN Lagos Chapter leadership

A decade of progressive service within the Association of Professional Women Engineers of Nigeria (APWEN), Lagos Chapter, a division of the Nigerian Society of Engineers.

2013–2015

Publicity Secretary

Supported chapter communications, publicity and member engagement.

2015–2019

General Secretary

Supported chapter administration, coordination and professional engagement. Activities included support for engineering outreach and participation in the 2018 Global Engineering Congress in London.

2019–2021

Assistant Chairman

Supported the Chairman in delivering chapter priorities, including mentoring, capacity-building, public lectures and professional development programmes.

2021–2023 · Inaugurated 10 March 2022

Chairman · 9th Chairperson of APWEN Lagos Chapter

Led chapter programmes focused on professional development, STEM engagement, mentorship, health awareness and community service.

Following relocation to the UK in July 2023, the Vice Chairman assumed onsite leadership during the transition. Formal handover was completed in the third week of August 2023.

Selected initiatives and community impact

Examples drawn from APWEN chapter reports and professional profile materials. These are presented as chapter initiatives delivered during the period of my leadership, not as sole individual achievements.

Girls’ STEM education and empowerment

Chapter programmes introduced schoolgirls to coding and artificial intelligence around the International Day of the Girl Child, in partnership with STEM Advance Foundation. The chapter also supported the Felicia Agubata Science Competition and Scholarship (FASCOS) initiative.

Mentorship and professional development

Supported mentoring and career-development activities, including the Vital Voices Global Mentoring Walk, technical learning opportunities, public lectures and professional engagement for women in engineering.

Breast cancer awareness and screening

The chapter's awareness programme on 30 October 2021 combined health education, a survivor testimony and breast and cervical screening activities.

Engineering outreach and inclusion

Engineering outreach with Black Women in Engineering in June 2022 focused on circular economy and sustainable management, helping connect engineering ideas with wider community and sustainability themes.

Voluntary blood donation initiative

An October 2022 public lecture and blood donation drive was delivered with partner organisations. The supplied speaker profile records 39 volunteers screened, 22 assessed as fit to donate and 22 pints intended for Massey Children’s Hospital.

Wider professional engagement

Chapter activities included stakeholder engagement with Yaba College of Technology, a job fair with Field Ready, school career talks, World Engineering Day discussions on the Sustainable Development Goals and recognition of women in engineering.

Evidence note: Chapter-level statistics and programmes are attributed to APWEN reporting. They are not presented as personal performance metrics. Engineering leadership and public service

My professional leadership has also included technical and quality responsibilities in construction and public-sector engineering.

Technical oversight and quality assurance

As a District Engineer and later in Inspectorate & Quality Control functions with Lagos State Government, I contributed to structural design review, building inspections, site supervision, compliance monitoring and quality assurance of construction personnel, materials and methods.

Green building and infrastructure

Contributed to eGIS and Green Building Initiative activities, including promoting EDGE values in infrastructure design and construction, preparing a BEME for the eGIS Archive and reviewing sewer and wastewater-management designs before construction approval.
