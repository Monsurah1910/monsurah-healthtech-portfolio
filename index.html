About

Connecting operational experience with digital improvement

My career began in engineering in 1998 and has included nearly two decades with Julius Berger Nigeria Plc, six years with the Lagos State Government, and leadership across the Association of Professional Women Engineers of Nigeria (APWEN), Lagos Chapter. My experience spans civil and structural engineering, site supervision, design review, procurement, warehouse and materials management, quality assurance and control, regulatory inspection, programme coordination and community engagement. Since relocating to the UK in July 2023, I have also gained experience in healthcare support and volunteering. I am developing my specialism in HealthTech Business Analysis through structured training and practical case-study work, with a focus on understanding workflows, clarifying stakeholder needs and documenting improvements that support safe, effective services.

Professional experience & leadership

Engineering, quality and professional leadership

My engineering career and professional leadership have shaped a practical approach to quality, collaboration, communication and continuous improvement.

Julius Berger Nigeria Plc | 1998–2017

Joined as an Engineering Trainee in 1998. Across nearly 16 years, my functional assignments covered engineering and site delivery, technical inspection, drafting, supervision and materials operations. My official employment record retained the designation Engineering Technician; the responsibilities below describe the work I undertook, not a sequence of formal promotions.

  • 1998–1999: Engineering Trainee.
  • 2000–2001: Soil and Concrete Inspector.
  • 2001–2002: AutoCAD Engineer; also undertook site engineering responsibilities during the wider 2001–2004 period.
  • 2001–2004: Site Engineer.
  • 2005–2007: Office Engineer.
  • 2007–2009: Site Supervisor.
  • 2009–2016: Site stores and materials operations across different project locations.
  • 2016–2017: Lagos Main Store, with Assistant Project Store Coordinator responsibilities.

Materials and stores responsibilities included receiving and issuing, stock control, procurement planning, inventory records, reconciliation and materials tracking using a Warehouse Management System (WMS).

The breadth of assignments also provided exposure to procurement, tendering, contract billing, quality inspection, construction supervision and site health and safety.

Lagos State Government | 2017–2023

Served in engineering and Inspectorate & Quality Control functions, including District Engineer assignments at Alimosho (2017–2018) and Ikoyi/Victoria Island (2018–2019), followed by Assistant Director responsibilities.

Work included structural design reviews for building approval documentation, site supervision against approved designs, building inspections, monitoring and enforcement, and quality assurance and control of personnel, materials and construction methods. Further assignments included eGIS and Green Building Initiative activities, BEME preparation for the eGIS Archive, and design checks for sewer and wastewater-management elements at the Lagos State Wastewater Management Office.

Additional work with Lagos State Government / LASBCA

  • Supported building-control, inspection and quality activities and prepared technical reports.
  • Led structural engineering activities within assigned operational areas.
  • Contributed to structural coordination and redesign of the LASBCA office development in Ikeja.

These roles developed transferable strengths in regulatory compliance, quality governance, technical review, risk awareness and stakeholder coordination.
