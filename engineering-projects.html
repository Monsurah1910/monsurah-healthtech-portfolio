Selected Engineering Projects & Technical Contributions

A selection of project assignments from my work with Julius Berger Nigeria Plc and the Lagos State Government, reflecting experience in site engineering, technical coordination, drawing management, building control and infrastructure design review.

These project summaries are drawn from my professional profile and career records. They describe my recorded roles and contributions, distinguishing individual responsibilities from wider team activities.

Julius Berger Nigeria Plc · 2001–2003

450-seat Faculty of Engineering Auditorium — University of Lagos

Served as Site Engineer on the construction of the 450-seat auditorium at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Lagos.

Contribution: site engineering and construction delivery.

Julius Berger Nigeria Plc · 2003–2005

Digitisation of company as-built drawing archives

Participated as part of the sustainability team converting archived as-built drawings for company offices, project sites and quarries in Lagos into electronic copies using AutoCAD.

Contribution: technical drawing conversion and archive digitisation within a team initiative.

Lagos State Government · 2020–2023

LASBCA office development — Ikeja

My professional profile records a project design-lead role for the LASBCA office building. My additional account describes structural coordination and redesign contributions to the office development.

Contribution: project design leadership and structural coordination/redesign, as described in my professional records.

Lagos State Government · 2022–2023

eGIS Archive and MPP&UD office improvement

Prepared the Bill of Engineering Measurement and Evaluation (BEME) for construction of the eGIS Archive and supervised internal improvement works, including the facelift of the MPP&UD entrance.

Contribution: preparation of project cost documentation and supervision of internal improvement works.

Lagos State Wastewater Management Office · 2023

Sewer and wastewater infrastructure design reviews

At the Design Office, carried out design checks and reviews of sewer networks and other wastewater-management elements before construction approval.

Contribution: technical design review in support of pre-construction scrutiny.
